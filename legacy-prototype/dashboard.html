STEP 03 · LEARN

What this may mean

When a seller does not deliver as promised, keeping proof of the transaction and your attempts to resolve it can help you ask for a remedy through the seller's grievance process or an appropriate consumer mechanism. The exact route depends on the facts.

iImportant: This is general information, not a conclusion about your legal rights or outcome.
