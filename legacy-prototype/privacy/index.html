PRIVACY

Privacy should be part of the product.

NyayaSetu AI is designed around collecting less, protecting more, and exposing nothing unnecessarily.

What we collect

Only information needed for the requested feature. Avoid sharing identity details unless they are necessary to understand your situation.

Documents

In this demo, supported files are read in your browser and are not saved or sent to an AI provider. Production storage must be private, authenticated, encrypted, and user-owned.

AI usage

Production requests should minimize and redact personal information, disclose when data leaves the app, and treat uploaded documents and retrieved content as untrusted input.

Your controls

Users should be able to delete documents and personal data, log out securely, and understand what is stored and why.
